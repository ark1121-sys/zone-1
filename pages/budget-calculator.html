What is a 50/30/20 Budget Calculator?

A 50/30/20 Budget Calculator is a simple yet powerful financial tool based on the popular "50/30/20 rule" for personal money management. This strategy helps you divide your after-tax income into three distinct categories: Needs, Wants, and Savings/Debt Repayment.

By allocating 50% of your income to necessities (rent, groceries, utilities), 30% to lifestyle choices (entertainment, dining out), and 20% to your financial future (savings, investments), you create a balanced approach to spending and saving. This calculator instantly computes these amounts, providing a clear visual representation through a pie chart to help you stay on track with your financial goals and achieve long-term stability.
