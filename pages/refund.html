Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please read this policy carefully before subscribing to ZONE PRO TRADER.

1. No Refund Policy

Zone Pro Trader offers digital products and immediate access to trading tools. Because of the nature of digital services, we have a strict No Refund Policy.

  • Once you activate your subscription, we cannot provide refunds for any reason.
  • We do not offer partial refunds for unused time in your subscription period.
  • Please evaluate the platform and think carefully before subscribing.

2. Cancellation Policy

While we do not offer refunds, you are free to cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges.

How to Cancel:

  • You can cancel your subscription directly using your Bank App or UPI App (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, etc.).
  • Navigate to the "Auto-Pay" or "Mandates" section in your UPI app and cancel the mandate for Zone Pro Trader.
  • You do not need to contact us to cancel—you have full control via your payment method.

Note: If you cancel, you can still use the tools until your current paid period ends. After that, your access will stop automatically.

3. Payment Errors

If a technical error occurs (for example, if you were charged twice for the same month due to a server glitch), we will investigate and refund the extra amount.

In such cases, please contact us immediately on WhatsApp with your payment proof.
