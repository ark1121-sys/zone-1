Privacy Policy

At ZONE PRO TRADER, we value your trust. This document explains what information we collect and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect basic information from you:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, and phone number when you sign up.
  • Trading Data: The trades, notes, and journals you enter into our system.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you use our website to help us improve user experience.

2. Payment Information

We take your financial security seriously.

  • We do NOT store your credit card, debit card, or banking passwords on our servers.
  • All payments are processed securely through Razorpay, a trusted third-party payment gateway.
  • Razorpay handles your payment data according to their own strict security standards.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for the following purposes:

  • To allow you to access your trading journal and dashboard.
  • To process your subscription payments.
  • To send you important updates regarding your account or our services.
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.

Note: We do NOT sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement strong security measures to protect your account. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your login credentials private.

5. Cookies

We use cookies (small text files) to keep you logged in and to remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features of the platform may not work correctly.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We recommend checking this page periodically for any changes.
